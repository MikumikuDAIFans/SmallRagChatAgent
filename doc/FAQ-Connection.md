This guide helps you connect your device and troubleshoot common issues in the current project. Follow the steps—no technical background required.

## How Do I Connect My CNC Machine?

- USB Connection (recommended)

  - Use the device’s included or a reliable data cable to connect the machine directly to your computer.
  - Open ArtiMaker Home and connect the device in the right-side Device panel.
  - After connection, click X, Y, Z below to run a simple test and confirm the machine responds.

- WiFi Connection (same network)
  - Connect both the device and the computer to the same wireless network.
  - In ArtiMaker, select “WiFi connection”, enter the IP as prompted, and connect.
  - After a successful connection, run a quick X, Y, Z test to confirm proper operation.

## The Device Is Not Recognized / Port Not Found

- Initial Checks

  - Confirm the driver is installed: on the Home page click “Install Driver” to install the appropriate driver.
  - Try different USB ports or a different cable; prefer short, reliable cables.
  - Replug the device and wait briefly before retrying.

- In the Software

  - Open ArtiMaker, in the right-side Device panel click ![240a0c4a-16b5-4e98-953d-e6393294faf7.png](/faq/240a0c4a-16b5-4e98-953d-e6393294faf7.png) to refresh, then select the device again.
  - Close other programs that might occupy the device to avoid connection conflicts.

- If Still Not Recognized
  - Restart the browser or computer and try again.
  - Try on another computer to rule out device hardware issues.

## USB Connection Is Unstable or Frequently Disconnects

- Connection Method

  - Connect directly to the computer; avoid docks, adapters, or hubs.
  - Use a shorter, sturdier cable and avoid pulling or bending.

- Environment

  - Keep the desk tidy and the device placed firmly; avoid tension on the cable.
  - Stay away from strong interference sources; maintain a quiet, orderly workspace.

- Operating Tips
  - After connecting, start with small-area, low-load tests and increase gradually.
  - Close unnecessary background programs to reduce system lag effects.

## The Machine Does Not Respond After Connection

- Basic Checks

  - Confirm “Connected” is shown in the software and the correct device is selected.
  - Run a small test movement and check for feedback.

- Safety and Status

  - Check that the emergency stop is released, the door or cover is closed, and the device is in a ready state.
  - If the device shows protection or alarms, reset or clear them before retrying.

- Retry
  - Disconnect and reconnect the device, or restart the browser and try again.
  - Return to Device and validate with the simplest motion first.

## How to Set Up WiFi Connection

- Preparation

  - Ensure the device and computer are on the same wireless network and the network is stable.

- In the App

  - Open ArtiMaker “Device” and select “WiFi”.
  - Enter the IP as prompted and connect.
  - After successful connection, run a simple X, Y, Z test.

- If Unstable
  - Keep the device close to the router to avoid weak signal or congestion.
  - If instability persists, prefer USB connection for machining or debugging.
