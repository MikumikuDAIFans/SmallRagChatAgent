### About ArtiMaker 
ArtiMaker — Turn your maker dream into reality. ArtiMaker is dedicated to empowering hobbyists, makers, and workshops with intuitive, all‑in‑one CNC software that transforms ideas into finished creations seamlessly. The platform covers the entire journey of design, making, and sharing, providing a complete workflow in one place. With support for mainstream CNC controllers and a wide range of toolpaths, ArtiMaker integrates AI‑powered features, rich template libraries, and smart parameter settings—making it easy for beginners to get started while enabling professionals to create with efficiency and precision. Through its unified “Design · Make · Share” experience, ArtiMaker is inspiring creators worldwide to truly Create without limits. 

### Sign Up & Sign In Registration Process

1. Open the ArtiMaker registration page. 

2. Fill in the following information: ▸ Email ▸ Full Name ▸ Password 
3. Check the box to agree to the following policies: ▸ Privacy Policy ▸ Terms of Service ▸ Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) 
4. Click the Sign up button to submit your registration. 
5. The system will send a verification email to the address you provided. 
6. Open the email and click the verification button to be redirected to ArtiMaker and automatically complete registration and login.



### Login Process

Option 1: Email Login
1. Open the ArtiMaker login page. 
2. Enter your Email and Password.
3. Optional: Check Remember me to stay signed in. 
4. Click the Sign in button to log in. 

Option 2: Google Login 
▸ ▸ Click the Sign in with Google button. The system will automatically register and log you in using your Google account—no additional information required. Frequently Asked Questions 
▸ Forgot your password? Click the Forgot password? link to reset it. 
▸ ▸ Already have an account? Click Sign on the registration page to go to the login screen. Don’t have an account? Click Sign up on the login page to go to the registration screen.
