# Credits Guide

## 💎 Credits Overview

ArtiMaker Credits is our growth incentive system designed for creators. You can earn credit rewards, use AI and system benefits, and continuously advance on your creator journey.

---

## 🎯 How to Earn Credits

### 📚 Earning Credits

| Action | Credit Reward | Description |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Complete Registration | **200 Credits** | Permanently valid |
| Complete daily check-in | **10 Credits** | Credits valid for 1 day |

## 🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do credits expire?**  
A: Regular credits are permanently valid. Special credits from event gifts may have an expiration date, which will be clearly marked when obtained.

**Q: Can redeemed resources be transferred?**  
A: Digital resources are bound to the account and do not support transfer.

**Q: Will the credit rules be updated?**  
A: We will optimize the credit system based on feedback. Major adjustments will be announced in advance, and already obtained credits will not be affected.
